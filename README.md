Fictional wargame organised by Sciences Po Cybersecurity Association on December 5th, 2026. The game includes classical strategic choices for the "Government Team" followed by negotiation rounds, as well as CTFs challenges for the "Cyber Teams". 
The CTFs are related to OSINT investigations and cybersecurity, and are designed to provide educational value. 

Players are divided in four teams, corresponding to two fictional countries and two types of teams, government and cyber. Each country starts with certain stats on different dimensions: cyber, economic, military, etc. Through government strategic choices, negotiation agreements and cyber challenges, the two countries can increase their own stats or decrease those of the adversary. The winning team is the one accumulating the most points overall, on all stats. 

The cyber challenges mainly use in-game, fictional resources. Occasionally, real OSINT tools may be needed to solve the challenges. 

The published website can be accessed here : https://celestinacopil.github.io/wargame2026/ .
