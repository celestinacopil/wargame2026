About the data

IMO number: a permanent 7-digit ship identifier. It stays the same when a ship changes name, flag or owner. The last digit is a check digit.

MMSI: the 9-digit radio identity broadcast by a ship's AIS transponder. The first three digits identify the flag state.

AIS positions: positions are received from coastal and satellite AIS stations. A ship can switch its transponder off, and a gap in the data is shown as such.

VesselTrace is a fictional training database created for the Arctic Crisis wargame. All vessels, companies, identifiers and positions are invented.
