Units

Distances at sea are measured in nautical miles (nm). 1 nautical mile = 1.852 km.

Accepted formats: degrees and decimal minutes (72°41.00′N, 72 41.0 N) or decimal degrees (72.6833). South latitudes and west longitudes are written with S / W or as negative numbers.
