Instructions

Belvaria's first move: get inside the Darsenian Coast Guard (DCG) to watch their investigation and find who leaked.

You cannot break in by force. Instead, trick a DCG insider into clicking a link that looks like their real staff portal. This is called phishing.

Do recon only, using public sources and a leaked DCG file. Use Seekr to find them. Then build the lure.

You need three things:

  1. Sender to impersonate: a DCG person whose emails with links look normal to staff.
  2. Look-alike domain: a separate domain that looks like the real staff portal.
  3. Pretext: the event that makes staff act fast.

Flag formats: FLAG{l.irsdottir} style. For the domain, write the full domain. For the pretext, join words with an underscore.

Everything stays inside the game. Do not register a real domain and do not send a real message.

Evidence

  • Seekr, the search engine
