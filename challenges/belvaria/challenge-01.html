Instructions

Belvaria's first move: get inside the Darsenian Coast Guard (DCG) to watch their investigation and find who leaked.

You cannot break in by force. Instead, trick a DCG insider into clicking a link that looks like their real staff portal. This is called phishing.

Do recon only, using public sources and a leaked DCG file. Use Seekr to find them. Then build the lure in the workbench below.

You need three things: a sender to impersonate, a look-alike domain, and a pretext (the event that makes staff act fast). Watch the two meters: you want high credibility and low detection risk.

Everything stays inside the game. Do not register a real domain and do not send a real message.

Evidence

  • Seekr, the search engine

Attacker's workbench

Phishing kit. Fictional. Nothing here sends a real message.

Pick the person whose emails with links look normal to DCG staff.

2. Domain: register a look-alike

The real staff portal is portal.dcg.dr. Register a different domain that looks like it.
