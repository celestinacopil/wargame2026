Instructions

You want DCG staff to land on a fake login that looks like their real one. Using DCG's public pages only, work out three things and submit the third as your flag:

  1. DCG's official website domain.
  2. DCG's real staff portal address (a subdomain of it).
  3. A look-alike domain you could register that staff might confuse with the real portal.

Everything stays inside the game. Do not register a real domain and do not send a real message.

Flag format: FLAG{look-alike-domain}

How look-alike domains work

The real staff portal is a subdomain of the real domain (word + dot + domain), controlled by the real owner. A look-alike is a separate domain you register that reuses the same words in a different shape.

To build one from a subdomain: take the subdomain word, move it to the front, join it to the main name with a hyphen, keep the ending.

Example (a different company): real portal mail.northstar.dr becomes look-alike northstar-mail.dr.
