Instructions

Both teams race to seize a shared offshore control panel. It holds a photo that identifies the ship that attacked the Aurora. The first team to crack the panel PIN and take control gets the proof. The other team is locked out of it for the rest of the phase.

The panel PIN is the time (UTC, four digits, no colon) when investigators confirmed the location of the explosion.

You can try as many times as you need. A wrong PIN has no penalty.

Evidence

  • Official report from Darsenia's Coast Guard
