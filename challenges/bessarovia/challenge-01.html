Build the lure

Available

Instructions

Bessarovia's first move: get inside the Aldeian Coast Guard (ACG) to watch their investigation and find who leaked.

You cannot break in by force. Instead, trick a ACG insider into clicking a link that looks like their real staff portal. This is called phishing.

Do recon only, using public sources and a leaked ACG file. Use Seekr to find them. Then build the lure in the Attacker's Workbench.

You need three things: a sender to impersonate, a look-alike domain, and a pretext (the event that makes staff act fast). The workbench tells you when your build is convincing. Then come back here and submit your three findings.

Write each flag as FLAG{your answer}. For the sender use first-initial.lastname, for the domain write the full domain, and for the pretext join the words with an underscore.

Everything stays inside the game. Do not register a real domain and do not send a real message.

Evidence

  • Seekr, the search engine

Backup: submit your findings by hand

Deploying the lure in the Attacker's Workbench records your findings automatically. Only use the form below if that didn't work: submit each objective by hand.

Submissions are recorded for the organisers. Accepted progress is also saved in this browser.

Lure deployed

Lure sent. Your email will reach a ACG mailbox. You only win if Aldeia clicks or fails to report it.

While you are inside, you notice a ACG officer has been messaging an outside Twitter account. Your next job: identify them.
