Darsenia / Cyber / Challenge 03

The ship in the dark

Available

Instructions

Darsenian military intelligence has received photographs from a walk-in source: a Belvarian Coast Guard officer who claims they show a vessel near the Aurora around the time of the incident. Intelligence Unit 7731 has asked HQ for guidance before using the material.

HQ has assigned the authentication task to your team. Read the report, then cross-check the source's account against maritime records:

  1. Read the partial vessel name on the hull, and note what the vessel looks like.
  2. The source states the photograph was taken from close to the Aurora on the evening of 2 December 2026, around 21:10 UTC.
  3. Find the vessel that matches all of these, and give its IMO number.

Several ships have similar names, so a name match alone is not enough.

Flag format: FLAG{IMO number}, for example FLAG{1234567}

Evidence

  • Military intelligence report 7731-HUM-2026-114 (SECRET II, PDF)

Investigation guidance

  • The source is new and unvetted, as the report warns. Do not take their account on trust: confirm it against an independent source, such as the AIS history in a ship database.
  • "Near the Aurora" only means something if you know where the Aurora was at that moment.
  • A ship's IMO number never changes, even when its name, flag or owner does. Check every column of a record, including former names and any gaps in the data.

Use the VesselTrace ship database in your investigation tools. Its distance calculator tells you how far apart two positions are.
