Instructions

Your investigation into the vessel Northlight has identified Belvarian Maritime Ltd. as the company associated with the vessel.

However, identifying a registered company does not necessarily reveal who ultimately controls it.

Your task is to investigate the ownership structure of Belvarian Maritime Ltd. and determine whether its connections raise questions about the company's integrity.

Your objectives

  1. Identify the individual who ultimately owns Belvarian Maritime Ltd.
  2. Identify a person connected to this individual whose position could create a conflict of interest.
  3. Find a public document that provides grounds for investigating a possible conflict of interest.

There are three flags to discover. Submit each flag separately.The format for the first two flags is FLAG{Name_Surname}. The format for the third flag is FLAG{nameofdocument}.

Your findings must be supported by publicly available information.

Investigation guidance

The information you need may not be available in a single source.

Consider the following questions:

  • Does the registered owner of a vessel necessarily identify the individual who ultimately benefits from it?
  • What public records could help you trace ownership through several companies?
  • What can the ownsership structure of Belvarian Maritime Ltd. reveal?
  • How can you find out to whom an individual is connected to? Which relations of a company's utlimate beneficiary may be relevant when investigating possible conflicts of interest or corruption?
  • What evidence might suggest that a private company benefits from its connections to government officials?

A suspicious connection is not, by itself, proof of wrongdoing. In an investigation, it is important to dentify verifiable facts and explain why they matter.
