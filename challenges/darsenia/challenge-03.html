Instructions

You have identified Belvarian Maritime Ltd. as the company that registered the vessel Northlight. However, the true beneficiary of a company is often hidden behind parent companies, This challenge asks you to advance the investigation. Your first mission is to find out the true owner of the vessel. Who owns Belvarian Maritime Ltd.,truly? Once you found out the person behind Belvarian Maritime Ltd., what can you say about their closed ones? Any family members or friends that may have a conflict of interest if they were found out to be involved with Maritime Holding Ltd.? Finally, can you find out any information that makes you think corruption is involved? For this challenge, you need to discover 3 FLAGS: 1. The name of the parent company's owner (format: FLAG{Name_Lastname} 2. The name of the person close to the parent company's owner, which may have a conflict of interest due to the connection he/she has with the person. (format: FLAG{Name_Lastname} 3. The name of the document that allows you to suspect that there is corruption involved. (format: FLAG{example.html}
