Instructions

Review the available reporting and official statements about the Aurora incident. Try to understand what happened, and notice the conflicting narratives surrounding the incident. Identify the details which seem suspicious. Which paragraph from which source suggests that someone knew specific details about the incident before they became publicly available?

The flag should contain the source name and the number of the relevant paragraph. Count only the main article paragraphs, starting at 1. Do not count the subtitle or the headings.

Flag format: FLAG{nameofsource_paragraph_0}

Investigation guidelines

Use the fictional search engine provided in the investigation toolkit. Choose to read the relevant sources. Make sure to read Belvarian, Darsenian, as well as international sources. Official reports may also be useful. Pay attention to publication times, for example by checking past versions of certain websites...
