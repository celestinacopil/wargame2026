ARCTIC CRISIS / ROUND 1 / SHARED SCENARIO BRIEF

The offshore reserve negotiations

Darsenia and Belvaria share a disputed maritime boundary. Their independence treaty did not fully specify the demarcation. The discovery of a shared offshore oil reserve has made this dispute urgent.

Agreed scenario facts

  • The reserve extends into both countries' undisputed maritime areas and the disputed zone.
  • Darsenia depends heavily on trade with Belvaria. It sees the reserve as a route towards energy security and greater economic independence.
  • Belvaria seeks a larger share of the oil revenues and greater regional influence.
  • Darsenia's civilian survey vessel Aurora suffered an explosion. Its cause and responsibility remain under investigation.

Issues to negotiate

  • Revenue and cost sharing. The scenario describes Darsenia arguing for an 80/20 split and Belvaria seeking a 60/40 split in its own favour. These are negotiating positions, not agreed geological measurements.
  • The operating company. Discuss ownership, independence, financing, access to data and oversight.
  • A provisional operating arrangement. Could the parties cooperate while reserving their positions on the final boundary?
  • A review mechanism. How would better reservoir data change shares, costs or operating responsibilities?
  • Safety and investigation access. What guarantees would protect civilian crews and preserve evidence?

Prepare a reasoned position

State a clear claim, cite a scenario fact, explain the link and identify its strategic effect. When responding to an accusation, address its evidence directly. Distinguish suspicion, evidence of sabotage and evidence of state responsibility.

Record any agreement with its conditions, time limits and verification method. The organisers decide how an agreement affects subsequent action choices.
