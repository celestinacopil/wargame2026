Drawing the Maritime Border

Duration: 20 minutes

1. Situation

Aldeia and Bessarovia disagree about the location of their maritime border. A large oil field has recently been discovered in the disputed area. Both countries want to control as much of it as possible. The two countries propose different borders: Aldeia wants the maritime border to continue diagonally from the land border. Under this proposal, Aldeia would control 90% of the oil field. Bessarovia wants the maritime border to follow a line of latitude. Under this proposal, Bessarovia would control 90% of the oil field. The oil field crosses both countries' maritime areas, regardless of which border is chosen. Neither country can control 100% of it. Currently, 80% of the oil field is in disputed waters. Neither country can legally drill there without an agreement.

2. Your Task

You have 20 minutes to negotiate a maritime border.

You can agree on either country's proposed border, draw a compromise line, or propose another solution.

You may offer concessions to reach an agreement, including:

  • A larger share of future oil profits for the other country.
  • Trade or economic benefits.
  • A one-time financial payment.
  • Other diplomatic or political concessions.

Important: This round is about the maritime border, NOT about how the oil field will be managed. You will negotiate the management of the oil field in Round 2. However, the border you agree on now will influence the negotiations in Round 2. For example, if the border gives Aldeia 60% of the oil field and Bessarovia 40%, this could become the basis for a 60/40 division of profits. The two countries can nevertheless negotiate a different division.

3. Rules & Scoring

If you reach an agreement:

  • Both countries gain +5 diplomatic points.
  • Each country gains or loses domestic politics points depending on its share of the oil field.
  • The agreement will influence negotiations about oil profits in Round 2, which will have a direct effect on your future economic points!

Domestic politics points are calculated as follows:

5 − (90 − your percentage of the oil field) ÷ 10

If you do NOT reach an agreement:

Both countries lose:

  • −5 economic points
  • −5 diplomatic points
  • −3 domestic politics points

The maritime border remains disputed. Neither country can legally drill in the disputed area.

You can continue negotiating the border in Round 2, or explore other ways to manage the oil field without agreeing on a border.

4. Important Information

  • An agreement about the border does not automatically determine how oil profits will be shared. This will be negotiated in Round 2.
  • A border agreement can be renegotiated in Round 2, but doing so costs 5 diplomatic points.
  • Countries may still attempt illegal drilling during the Actions Phase, but this may have consequences.
  • Promises made during negotiations are not automatically enforceable. A country may decide not to honour its commitments.
  • If outside-of-game promises are made, such as "a beer after the game", they will definitely not be enforced by the organisers, but feel free to try :)
  • Any agreed financial transfers will be applied to the countries' budgets before the next Actions Phase.

5. End of Round

Before the 20 minutes are over, inform the organisers whether you have reached an agreement.

If you have, submit your agreed maritime border and any additional concessions or commitments.

Remember: Your ideal outcome is 90% of the oil field, but failing to reach an agreement also has a cost.
