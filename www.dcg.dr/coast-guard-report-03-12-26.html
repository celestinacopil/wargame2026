Aurora incident: Location of the initial explosion confirmed

Reference: DMA/2026/118/02

Published: 18 November 2026, 08:30 UTC

Status: Preliminary findings. Investigation ongoing.

The Darsenian Maritime Authority provides the following update on the explosion aboard the scientific vessel Aurora at approximately 06:40 UTC today.

1. Location of the initial explosion

An initial review of crew statements, onboard alarm records and photographs transmitted from the vessel indicates that the explosion began in the compartment housing the vessel’s survey equipment.

Investigators confirmed this location at 08:10 UTC. The subsequent fire affected adjacent spaces.

2. Public release of this finding

This statement, published at 08:30 UTC, is the first official public confirmation of the compartment in which the explosion began.

Before this statement, neither the Authority nor any other Darsenian government body had publicly identified the affected compartment. No official press briefing, public incident notice or media response had provided that detail.

The Authority’s initial statement at 06:55 UTC confirmed the explosion, evacuation, reported injuries and condition of the vessel. It stated that the cause and precise location of the explosion aboard the vessel had not been established.

The Authority cannot account for information obtained by news organisations from unofficial sources. Any earlier media account identifying a specific compartment was not based on an authorised public release by Darsenian authorities.

3. Cause remains undetermined

Confirmation of the location does not establish the cause. Investigators have not determined whether the explosion resulted from a technical failure or deliberate interference.

Claims that an electrical fault caused the explosion remain unverified. Further inspection and examination of the vessel’s equipment are required.

4. Incident and information timeline

Time, UTC Event
06:40 Approximate time of the explosion.
06:52 Distress message received by maritime authorities.
06:55 Initial public statement issued. The cause and precise location aboard the vessel were not established.
08:10 Investigators confirmed the survey equipment compartment as the location of the initial explosion.
08:30 This technical update published, identifying the compartment publicly for the first time through official channels.

5. Crew and vessel

All crew members are accounted for. Three people were injured and are receiving medical care. The vessel remains afloat. Survey operations are suspended.
