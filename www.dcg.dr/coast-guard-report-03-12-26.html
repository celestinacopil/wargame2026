Public statement / Preliminary technical update

Aurora incident: Location of the initial explosion confirmed

Reference: DCG/2026/118/02

Published: 3 December 2026, 08:30 UTC

Status: Preliminary findings. Investigation ongoing.

The Darsenian Coast Guard provides the following update on the explosion aboard the scientific vessel Aurora at approximately 06:40 UTC today.

1. Location of the initial explosion

An initial review of crew statements, onboard alarm records and photographs transmitted from the vessel indicates that the explosion began in the compartment housing the vessel’s survey equipment.

Investigators confirmed this location at 08:10 UTC. The subsequent fire affected adjacent spaces.

2. Cause remains undetermined

Investigators have not determined whether the explosion resulted from a technical failure or deliberate interference.

Claims that an electrical fault caused the explosion remain unverified. Further inspection and examination of the vessel’s equipment are required.

3. Crew and vessel

All crew members are accounted for. Three people were injured and are receiving medical care. The vessel remains afloat. Survey operations are suspended.

4. Incident and information timeline

Time, UTC Event
06:40 Approximate time of the explosion.
06:42 Distress message received by maritime authorities.
06:55 Initial public statement issued. The cause and precise location aboard the vessel were not established.
08:10 Investigators confirmed the survey equipment compartment as the location of the initial explosion.
