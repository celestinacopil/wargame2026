**REPUBLIC OF DARSENIA** **DARSENIAN COAST GUARD** **Initial statement: Incident aboard the scientific vessel Aurora** **3 December 2026 | 06:55 UTC** **Reference: DCG/2026/1203/01** The Darsenian Coast Guard received a distress message at 06:52 UTC from the Darsenian scientific vessel *Aurora*, reporting an explosion at approximately 06:40 UTC, followed by a fire. The crew has evacuated. All crew members are accounted for. Three people are reported injured. The vessel remains afloat. The *Aurora* was conducting a civilian geological survey near the disputed maritime boundary. Emergency assistance is being coordinated. Further information will be released when verified. **Office of Public Information** Darsenian Coast Guard
