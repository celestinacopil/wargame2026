Explosion aboard Darsenian research vessel injures three

Crew evacuates the Aurora near a disputed offshore oil reserve. Authorities say the cause remains unknown.

By Elena Voss and Mikhail Berg | Deuters
Published November 18, 2026 · 07:05 UTC
Updated November 18, 2026 · 07:05 UTC
  • Explosion reported at approximately 06:40 UTC
  • Three crew members injured; research vessel remains afloat
  • Authorities have not established the cause
NORTH COAST, Nov 18 (Deuters) — An explosion aboard the Darsenian scientific vessel Aurora injured three crew members early on Wednesday, maritime authorities said, interrupting a research mission near an offshore oil reserve claimed in part by both Darsenia and Belvaria.

The explosion occurred at approximately 06:40 UTC and was followed by a fire. The crew evacuated the vessel, which remains afloat, Darsenian maritime authorities said in an initial statement.

All crew members have been accounted for. Authorities have not released details about the severity of the injuries.

The cause of the explosion and its precise location aboard the vessel have not been established, the statement said. A technical inspection will be required once conditions permit safe access.

Investigation pending

“Our immediate priority is the safety of the crew and the stabilization of the vessel,” a spokesperson for Darsenia’s maritime authorities said. “It is too early to determine what caused the explosion.”

There was no immediate evidence available to Deuters that would establish whether the incident resulted from an equipment failure or deliberate interference.

Darsenia’s government said the vessel was carrying out civilian geological research. Belvarian authorities had not issued a formal response at the time of publication.

A disputed oil reserve

Darsenia and Belvaria have competing claims over part of their maritime boundary. The discovery of an offshore oil reserve extending across the area has increased pressure on both governments to reach an agreement.

The countries have not agreed on operating conditions for surveys in the disputed zone or on arrangements for the future development of the reserve.

The interruption of the Aurora’s mission could further complicate those discussions. Neither government has announced when research operations might resume.
