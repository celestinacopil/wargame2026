Scope of services

The contract covers coastal transportation of crude oil between designated production and terminal facilities, together with associated terminal handling, scheduling and logistics services.

The procurement forms part of the National Energy Infrastructure Programme, which supports the development and operation of strategic energy transport capacity.

Procurement record

The Ministry records that two tenders were received under the restricted procedure. Following evaluation, Belvarian Maritime Ltd. was selected as the successful tenderer.

Award notice published by the Procurement Directorate on 22 December 2025.

This notice records the published procurement outcome. It does not contain the full evaluation report, declarations of interest or internal approval file. Requests for further procurement records may be submitted to the Ministry's Public Information Office.
